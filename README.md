# Inbound Solution – Growth & Business Development
https://inbound-solution.fr/

## Overview

Inbound Solution is a French SEO agency where I managed the company's growth strategy across acquisition, sales, partnerships and marketing.

My role covered both the strategic and operational sides of growth, from identifying new acquisition opportunities to building the systems and partnerships needed to turn them into recurring business.

## What I Did

* Managed the overall growth and acquisition strategy
* Developed and managed B2B lead generation campaigns
* Built and managed a B2B affiliate network from scratch
* Identified, approached and onboarded new partners
* Developed commercial offers and negotiated partnership terms
* Managed outbound prospecting and sales
* Closed new business and negotiated contracts
* Developed SEO and content-driven acquisition strategies
* Managed client relationships and identified upselling opportunities
* Recruited and managed 3 collaborators
* Worked across international markets including France, Switzerland, Belgium, the UK, US and Netherlands

## Results

Over two years, I signed **29 clients** across France, Switzerland, Belgium, the United Kingdom, the United States and the Netherlands.

I managed the commercial process from prospecting through closing, while also developing new acquisition channels to diversify the company's growth.

## Building New Acquisition Channels

One of the main initiatives I developed was a B2B affiliate network.

There was no structured affiliate channel or existing partner network when I started. I developed the offer, identified relevant partners, conducted outreach, negotiated commercial terms and built the relationships.

The channel became the company's first structured affiliate acquisition channel, with reliable partners regularly referring new clients.
